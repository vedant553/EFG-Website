Service 08

Structural
Audit

We carry out scientific structural assessments using non-destructive testing methods to evaluate the integrity, residual life, and safety of existing buildings — delivering an authoritative report backed by engineering data.

Visual & Distress Survey

Mapping of cracks, spalling, settlement, and visible structural damage.

Non-Destructive Testing (NDT)

Rebound hammer, UPV pulse velocity, and carbonation depth tests.

Load Bearing Analysis

Calculation of residual structural capacity versus current usage loads.

Rehabilitation Report

A prioritised action plan with repair methodology and cost estimates.
