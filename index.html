About The Group

The Eight Fold Group

Eight Fold Group is a leading civil and landscape contractor with over two decades of experience operating across Maharashtra.

Formerly known as Sushant Enterprises, the company specializes in a wide range of engineering and contracting services, including civil construction, landscape design, interior contracting, and structural finishing.

With an elite team of professional engineers and project consultants, Eight Fold Group delivers high-quality, cost-effective solutions while ensuring strict adherence to timelines and safety standards. We are committed to building long-term trust through transparent communication and ensuring 100% customer satisfaction across residential, industrial, and commercial sectors.
